Help & Documentation

📚 Getting Started

Welcome to LuminaFlow! This platform helps you automate your LinkedIn outreach, WhatsApp messaging, and lead generation.

◐ LinkedIn Bot

  • Upload your LinkedIn session file or create a new session
  • Configure your personality and portfolio link
  • Daily limit: 150 connections per day for safety
  • Use test mode to verify functionality before going live

◆ WhatsApp Bot

  • Set your AI personality for conversations
  • Specify contacts to message (comma-separated)
  • Exclude specific contacts from automation
  • Upload bulk message files for mass outreach

◎ Lead Generation

  • Enter keywords to find relevant leads
  • Specify location for targeted searches
  • Set maximum number of leads to generate
  • View and export leads with detailed information

✓ Best Practices

  • Always respect LinkedIn's daily connection limits
  • Test bots in test mode before full deployment
  • Monitor logs regularly for any issues
  • Keep your session files secure and backed up
  • Download lead reports regularly for your records

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Keep your account credentials secure
  • Don't exceed LinkedIn's daily limits to avoid restrictions
  • Use professional and respectful messaging
  • Comply with all platform terms of service
